Read this before anything else. You said ₹16–17 lakh for the whole house and ₹10–12 lakh for neev plus ground floor. A 997 sq ft floor cannot be built and finished for that here, and I would rather say so now than let you find out at slab level. Site works alone take ₹5.8 lakh before one wall stands, because lifting a plot that floods to road level is expensive and invisible.

Three ways to make ₹12 lakh work for phase 1. One: build the full 997 sq ft frame and slab but finish only the master bedroom, kitchen and one toilet — about ₹13.7 lakh. Two: shrink the ground floor to roughly 700 sq ft by pushing the bachelor bedroom and utility into phase 2, about ₹11.8 lakh, with columns still cast for the full plan. Three: keep the plan and stretch phase 1 across two working seasons. Before you dig. One trial pit, 6 ft deep, south-west corner, in dry weather. If water seeps in above 6 ft, pad footings will not serve and this becomes a raft. A day of labour decides a third of the structural cost.
